Why it exists

Writing a script for one creator used to take me 15 to 20 minutes, and every creator on a campaign needs their own, in their own voice. The brief stays the same. Only the voice changes.

That is repetitive work with a clear pattern, so I built a tool for it.

What goes in, what comes out

You set the brand or product, the key message, the platform (Stories, TikTok, Reels or Shorts), the video length (15, 30 or 60 seconds), the creator's tone and the style of the call to action: a soft mention, a promo code push, link in bio or an app install.

Back comes a script on a timeline. Each beat has the spoken line and a cue for on-screen text. A caption and hashtags come with it. Drafts can be saved to a library, opened in Google Docs or downloaded as a .doc file.

Decisions I made

  • It never returns nothing. Requests go through my own backend, a Cloudflare Worker that calls the Claude API. If the AI is unavailable, the tool falls back to local templates.
  • The script has to fit the clock. The prompt holds the text to a speaking-pace budget for the chosen length.
  • No invented facts. The model may not make up claims about the product that are not in the brief.
  • Disclosure stays a human job. The tool reminds you about the paid partnership label or #ad, and does not add it for you.
  • A draft is a draft. I still edit every one before it goes out, usually the hook.
