Why it exists

Creators send screenshots of their stats, and I used to retype the numbers into a spreadsheet. It is slow, and one mistyped digit ends up in a report the brand reads.

What goes in, what comes out

You upload the screenshot. The tool reads views, reach, likes, comments, shares and saves, and files them under the campaign and the creator.

The campaign view shows total views and reach, the average engagement rate and a written conclusion: top performers, anomalies, recommendations. The creator view is a profile across every campaign, with each post and its engagement rate, so you can see who delivers every time and not only once.

Decisions I made

  • A recognition error must not reach the brand. Every reading passes a validation layer. More likes than views, engagement above 100%, reach more than 20 times the follower count, a negative value: any of these sends the record to a manual review queue with the reason written out.
  • Clean records go straight through. The queue only holds what needs a person.
  • No reach metric, no guess. If the tool cannot read views, reach or impressions at all, it asks for manual input.
  • Creator profiles use no AI. They are local arithmetic, so they open instantly and cost nothing in API quota.
