tools

Tools I built for the routine parts of the job.

Each one came from a task I got tired of doing by hand: writing scripts, collecting screenshots for reports, sorting replies, searching for creators.

Brief → Script

live

Writing a script for one creator used to take me 15-20 minutes. This takes a brief and the creator's style and gives back a script draft with hook, body and call to action in seconds. It can do a whole campaign at once and export to Google Docs. I still edit every draft before it goes out, usually the hook.

live, team access

Creators send screenshots of their stats, and I used to retype the numbers into a spreadsheet. This reads the numbers from the screenshots, puts them in a review queue so I can check them against the image, and builds the report for the brand.

Inbox → Triage

live

After outreach, replies pile up and the good ones get lost. This sorts each reply by what the creator wants and how urgent it is, drafts an answer, logs it to a sheet, and leaves the draft in Gmail. Nothing gets sent without me.
