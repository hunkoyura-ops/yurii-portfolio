Why it exists

Building a shortlist by hand means hours of scrolling through profiles, checking audience size and engagement one creator at a time. The criteria are known before the search starts, so the search itself can be done by a tool.

Where it stands

This one is not finished. The preview above shows the result I am building toward: you set the platform, niche, audience size, engagement rate and location, and get back a ranked list with the best fit first.
